Drum engine

Each drum is a control point the whole map blends toward, so shaping it reshapes nearby sounds too.

How it works

  • Tap a step to switch it on or off. A new step takes the sound your cursor was last on; switching one off clears it completely, so tapping it back on starts fresh.
  • Hold the pad, then tap a step to give that step the sound under your finger.
  • Hold one or more steps, then touch the pad and those steps take the point you touched last.
  • Only one pointer? Select latches steps so the pad updates them without needing to hold them down. On a keyboard, hold 1 to 8 and Q to I for steps 1 to 16 while using the mouse on the pad, or focus the pad and use the arrow keys.
  • Hold a step, then tap another to copy the first step's sound onto the second.
  • Knobs: drag up or right to turn up, down or left to turn down. Hold Shift for fine moves, double-tap to reset.
  • Step lanes (the bars icon) add per-step odds: how often a step plays, how often (and how far) it strays from its saved X, Y or decay when it does.
  • Quantize sets how finely holding the pad retriggers (1/16 for a normal beat, finer for ratchets), live or while recording — and can be changed on the fly.
  • Record arms live recording: while it's playing, touching the pad writes your live position into whichever step is currently passing, overdub-style — steps you don't touch keep whatever they already had. A finer quantize setting overdubs ratchets into that step without changing its 16-step grid.
  • Choke makes the kit monophonic: a new hit cuts off whatever's still ringing.
  • Drum engine layers: body (pitched sine with FM), noise, metal (808-style hat squares), resonator (a struck physical model that morphs from string to drum skin to bar), then a filter, a wavefolder and drive.
  • Undo / redo (or Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z) step back and forward through edits — steps, lanes, kit and engine tweaks, all of it.
  • Each die randomizes by how far you drag it right (or tap a spot on its track) — for the sequence, the selected drum (on the drum-engine page), or the whole kit (on the kit page).
  • Swipe left or right, or use the icons beside the title, to switch between the pad, lanes, whole kit, drum engine and this page.
